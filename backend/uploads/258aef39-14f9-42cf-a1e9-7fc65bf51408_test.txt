You can ask a wide variety of questions to help you digest, analyze, or act on the content of a web page. Since I can see the text and layout of the page you have open, I can help with everything from quick summaries to deep dives.

Here are the main categories of questions you can ask:

1. Summarization and Extraction
"What are the 3 main takeaways from this article?"

"Can you summarize this page in two sentences for a beginner?"

"List all the dates and deadlines mentioned here."

"What are the product specifications or pricing tiers?"

2. Analysis and Comparison
"What is the author's main argument, and what evidence do they use?"

"Are there any biases or logical inconsistencies in this text?"

"How does the product on this page compare to [Competitor Name]?"

"Is the tone of this review generally positive or negative?"

3. Navigation and Search
"Where on this page does it mention the return policy?"

"Does this site provide a contact email or phone number?"

"Find the section that explains how to troubleshoot the 'Error 404' issue."

4. Writing and Content Creation
"Rewrite the second paragraph to be more professional."

"Based on this page, draft a LinkedIn post sharing these insights."

"Create five quiz questions based on this tutorial."

5. Translation and Explanation
"Explain the technical jargon used in the 'Specifications' section."

"Translate the 'About Us' section into Spanish."

"What does the term '[Specific Word]' mean in this specific context?"

Tip: The more specific you are, the better. Instead of asking "What is this about?", try "What does this page say about the cost of subscription?"
